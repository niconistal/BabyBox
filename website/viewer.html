The redesign

Five printed parts, soldered build

A compact box: a bottom tray for the Pi, a short body, and a top plate with a raised, sealed LED ring around a recessed hole — the figurine drops straight in and the RFID reader sits right beneath it. Two MX buttons wear big round keycaps. Held together with brass heat-set inserts — no breadboard.
